## 🌱 About GreenPlate

**GreenPlate** is a **web-based decentralized food surplus management and distribution platform** designed to reduce food waste while improving food accessibility within communities. The platform serves as a bridge between **food donors**, **recipient organizations**, and **volunteer couriers**, making food donation faster, smarter, and more transparent.

Restaurants, supermarkets, hotels, schools, and event organizers can easily post surplus food that is still safe for consumption. Accredited charities, food banks, and community organizations can browse or request available donations based on their needs, while volunteers receive optimized pickup and delivery assignments to ensure food reaches recipients before expiration.

By leveraging **real-time matching**, **intelligent distribution**, and **route optimization**, GreenPlate contributes to a more sustainable food ecosystem while providing valuable insights through analytics on rescued food, meals served, and environmental impact.

## ✨ Key Features

- 👤 **Multi-role User Management**
  - Administrator
  - Food Donor
  - Recipient Organization
  - Volunteer Courier

- 🍱 **Food Donation Management**
  - Create and manage food donations
  - Upload food images
  - Track quantity and expiration

- 🤝 **Smart Food Matching**
  - Automatically match donations with recipients
  - Prioritize urgency and food availability

- 🚚 **Volunteer Coordination**
  - Pickup and delivery scheduling
  - Real-time delivery tracking

- 🗺️ **Dynamic Route Optimization**
  - Faster and more efficient delivery routes
  - Reduced travel time and fuel consumption

- 📊 **Analytics Dashboard**
  - Food rescued
  - Meals provided
  - CO₂ emissions reduced
  - Donation trends
  - Community impact

- 🔔 **Real-time Notifications**
  - Donation requests
  - Pickup reminders
  - Delivery updates
  - Status changes

- 📄 **Reports & Audit Logs**
  - Donation history
  - User activity logs
  - Exportable reports

- 📱 **Responsive Web Application**
  - Desktop
  - Tablet
  - Mobile devices
